    Strategies

    A strategy is a specific idea from a source -- a chapter, a rule, a specific call -- that ideas can be tagged with.

      Import statements

      A monthly audit, not a loader. Most rows will be trades you already recorded — the job is finding the ones that are missing or wrong. Nothing is written until you say so.

      Several files for one account are staged together on purpose: a sell in one export is often covered by a buy in another, and staging them separately would orphan it.

      Execution efficiency

      What your plans said against what actually happened. Positive always means you did better than the plan. This is not a measure of whether a source picks well — that needs hundreds of trades before the noise settles. It measures whether the plan was followed, which needs only a handful.

      Against the market

      A source that returned 8% cannot be judged without knowing what the market did over the same days it was held. Measured trip by trip against the benchmark, not against a calendar year — a trade held nine days is compared against nine days.

      Notifications

      What your plans said to do, and what you decided. Nothing here acts on its own — and deciding late costs nothing, because an alert records the price and time it fired at.

              Brokerages

              The firms you hold accounts with. Renaming one is safe; the key it imports under never changes.

                Accounts

                Identified by brokerage and account number, which is how statements are labelled — and how a monthly import knows which account a file belongs to.

                  Exchanges

                  Exchanges are normally created automatically when a ticker is resolved. Add one manually only if you need to.

                    General

                    When the URL is set, a JSON payload is POSTed here every time a price alert fires -- a hook point for external automations (e.g. Home Assistant). If the target needs authentication (e.g. HA's long-lived access token), paste the full header value above, including the scheme -- it's sent as-is in the request's Authorization header. Leave blank if the endpoint doesn't need one.

                    Table Columns

                    Which columns each table shows, and in what order. Any table in the app with a "⚙ Columns" button is listed here too -- this is just a central place to find them, including ones added in future updates.
